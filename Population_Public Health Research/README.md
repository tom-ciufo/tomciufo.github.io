# Population Health GIS Mapping and Analysis

## Overview

This project uses GIS tools to visualize and analyze healthcare and population data. The goal is to identify geographic patterns in disease distribution and healthcare access.

---

## Objectives

* Create choropleth maps using health data
* Compare different classification methods
* Analyze spatial patterns in population health
* Support data-driven public health decisions

---

## Tools Used

* QGIS
* CSV datasets
* Shapefiles (spatial data)

---

## Methods

### Classification Techniques

* Quantile
* Equal Interval
* Jenks Natural Breaks

Each method displays data differently and impacts interpretation.

---

## Key Findings

* Higher population and case concentrations in urban areas
* Rural areas show lower density and fewer cases
* Classification methods significantly affect visualization results
* Jenks Natural Breaks provided the most realistic clustering

---

## Public Health Impact

GIS mapping helps:

* Identify high-risk areas
* Allocate healthcare resources effectively
* Support public health planning
* Improve population health outcomes
